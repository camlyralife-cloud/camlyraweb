What is Pre-Marital Counselling?

“A successful marriage requires falling in love many times, always with the same person.” – Steve Harvey

Pre-marital counselling is a structured therapy process that helps couples prepare for marriage by addressing expectations, values, communication patterns, and potential challenges before commitment.

Importance of Pre-Marital Counselling

  • Strengthens Relationship Skills: Improves communication and conflict resolution.
  • Promotes Understanding: Builds clarity about values and expectations.
  • Prevents Future Issues: Identifies potential conflicts early.
  • Builds Confidence: Helps couples feel prepared for marriage.
  • Encourages Commitment: Strengthens emotional bonding and trust.

Causes for Pre-Marital Counselling

  • Unresolved Issues: Existing conflicts before marriage.
  • Differences in Values: Misalignment in beliefs and life goals.
  • Communication Challenges: Difficulty expressing feelings clearly.
  • Expectations Gaps: Unclear assumptions about marriage roles.
  • Life Transition Preparation: Adjusting to married life responsibilities.
