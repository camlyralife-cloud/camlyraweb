About us

The people who will be in your corner.

Every member of the Calmyra team is selected not just for their clinical or coaching credentials, but for their ability to bring genuine care into every session. Our practitioners hold postgraduate qualifications, maintain active professional registrations, and work within a framework of ongoing clinical supervision — so the quality of your care never depends on who is having a good day.

Vision

A world where mental health care is as refined, accessible, and stigma-free as any other form of expert professional support.

Mission

To deliver personalised, evidence-based mental wellness — through clinical therapy, coaching, and assessment — to individuals and families who deserve care that matches the full complexity of their lives. In Dubai, in Bangalore, and everywhere in between.
